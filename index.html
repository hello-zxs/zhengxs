The through-line

A lens is a piece of geometry. A coordinate transformation is a material. Change the space, and light obeys.

I am a physics MSc student at Western University, working where transformation optics meets the bench: geodesic lenses, conformal mapping, and single-photon interference.

My BSc thesis at Xiamen University Malaysia asked a narrow question — what surface is equivalent to a Morse lens? — and answered it with a metric, a quadrature, and a ray tracer.
