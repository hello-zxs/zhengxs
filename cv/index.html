Thesis

Ray tracing and equivalent geodesic lenses for the Morse lens

2026

BSc (Hons) thesis · Xiamen University Malaysia

  • Proved that conformal Morse lenses are equivalent to the (A = B, C = 1) subclass of Cornbleet's general family, unifying the Cornbleet–Rinous (1981) and Šarbort–Tyc (2012) lines of work.
  • Constructed equivalent geodesic surfaces for the Luneburg, Eaton and intermediate Morse profiles, and verified them by ray tracing.
  • Built an end-to-end pipeline from index profile to surface metric to numerical ray paths.
